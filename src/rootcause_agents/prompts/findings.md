Summarise your investigation as structured findings.

Rules:
- `key_observations`: factual statements only (kind="fact"), each citing the evidence IDs (E-n) that show it. Include exact times, magnitudes and services.
- `timeline`: time-ordered events (earliest first) with evidence IDs.
- `gaps`: data you could not obtain (tool errors, timeouts, partial/stale data) and why it matters.
- `conflicts`: observations that contradict each other or the initial report.
- Never cite an evidence ID that did not appear in the tool results. Never speculate about root cause here.

Be brief: at most 6 key observations and 5 timeline entries, one sentence per item.
